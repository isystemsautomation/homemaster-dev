home-master.eu

Configurator, firmware & documentation

Everything for your HomeMaster devices in one place. Configure modules over USB-C, download firmware, and open datasheets, schematics and declarations of conformity — for the PLCs, the OpenTherm gateway and every module.

WebConfig needs desktop Chrome, Edge, Opera or any Chromium browser. Firefox only on Nightly with the Web Serial flag. Not supported in Safari.
Current stable, shipped on new modules Beta under testing, may change Old superseded, kept for existing units
