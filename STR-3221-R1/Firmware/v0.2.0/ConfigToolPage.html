Output Channels O1–O32 (0–255)

32 dimmable stair-LED channels via TLC59208F (8 per driver U9–U12).

Master & panic

Master level multiplies every channel and is applied last — after the profile, the output window and the curve. Panic drives every channel to the panic level and holds it there, ignoring the bus, until it is cleared.

Channel profiles

Per channel: how the holding-register value is read, the dimming curve, the output window and the ramp. Order of application is profile → min/max → curve → master level → ramp.

Groups

Eight logical groups, each a free selection of channels — they need not follow the four physical driver rails. A level written to any member is mirrored once to every channel sharing a group with it; overlapping groups do not loop, and the last write wins.

Scenes

Eight scenes of 32 levels. Save captures the levels the channels are commanded to right now; recall applies them. A scene can also be recalled from holding register 436, from a button or from an input.

Stairs

On-board sequencer. Mode is how a run starts (one-shot or hold while presence). Pattern is the drawing — sequential, all, wave, comet, center, random. Hydrates only after the stairs section arrives.

Heating
