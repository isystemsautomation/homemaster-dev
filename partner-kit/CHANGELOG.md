# Partner kit changelog

## 2026.09

- Initial public partner kit: eleven HomeMaster modules.
- Product descriptions, specification tables, WebP images, EU DoC and datasheets.
- Download archive: `homemaster-partner-kit-2026.09.zip`.
- Added `HomeMaster_Catalogue.pdf` — full product catalogue, one A4 page per module with purpose, specifications and differentiators, plus partner terms and compliance summary. Not included in the archive above; download separately.
- Added signed UK PSTI statements of compliance for MiniPLC, MicroPLC and OpenTherm Gateway (`PSTI_MiniPLC.pdf`, `PSTI_MicroPLC.pdf`, `PSTI_OpenthermGateway.pdf` in each module’s `doc/`). Required for a British distributor to place a connectable product on the GB market. The eight Modbus RTU slave modules are out of scope.
